## World Cup Historical Patterns

### Group Stage Goal Patterns
- Average goals per match (2022): 2.55
- Average goals per match (2018): 2.64
- Average goals per match (all-time): 2.52
- Games with 2+ goals: ~75%
- Games with 3+ goals: ~52%
- Games with 0-0 draws: ~7%

### Favorites in Group Stage
- Pot 1 teams win ~55% of group stage matches
- Pot 1 vs Pot 4 matches average 2.8 goals (favorites usually dominate)
- Upsets (Pot 4 beating Pot 1) happen in ~12% of matches

### Key Pattern: Opening Matches
- Teams in their first group match tend to be cautious
- Opening match goals per game: ~2.2 (slightly below average)
- Clean sheets more common in opening matches (~35%)
- Fewer cards in opening matches (referees and teams cautious)

### Key Pattern: Must-Win Matches
- Third group match when teams need a result: more goals, more cards
- Desperation leads to tactical gambles and open play
- These matches are best for attacking player picks

### Home Advantage (2026 Context)
- USA, Canada, Mexico have "home" advantage from crowd support
- CONCACAF teams (USA, Canada, Mexico, Panama, Haiti, Curacao) play in familiar time zones and conditions
- European teams may face jet lag and heat in summer matches
- South American teams handle North American conditions well
